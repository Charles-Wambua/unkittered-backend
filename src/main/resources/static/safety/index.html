Safety

Meet people, safely

Most people on Unkittered are here for exactly what you are. These habits help keep it that way, online and when you meet.

In danger right now? Call the emergency services first. Kenya 999 or 112 · United Kingdom 999 or 112 · United States 911. Then report the person to us.

Chatting

  • Keep the conversation in Unkittered until you feel confident.
  • Never send money, gift cards or crypto, whatever the story.
  • Don't share your home address, workplace or financial details.
  • Be wary of anyone who won't video call, or rushes to move off the app.

Meeting up

  • Meet somewhere public and busy, like a café, a park in daylight, or a pack event.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, who with, and when you'll check in.
  • Arrange your own transport there and back.
  • Stay in control of your drink and your belongings. Leave whenever you want.

Trust badges

  • Verified: a person checked their selfie against their photos.
  • Sterilised · verified: we checked a document they uploaded.
  • Sterilised, self-declared: they told us, but we haven't checked.
  • Badges help, but they're not background checks. Trust your instincts.

Report and block

  • Open their profile or chat, tap the menu, then Report or Block.
  • Blocking hides you from each other right away.
  • They're never told who reported them.
  • Can't use the app? Report by email.

Red flags

If something feels off, it probably is

Report anyone who does any of these. You won't be wasting our time.

  • Asks for money or helpEmergencies, investments, travel costs or "customs fees".
  • Pressures youTo meet somewhere private, share photos, or move to another app fast.
  • Story doesn't add upPhotos that look professional, details that change, or seems too good to be true.
  • Seems under 18Report straight away. Unkittered is for adults only.
