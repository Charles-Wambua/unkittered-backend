How it works

Three steps to your people

No fence-sitters and no awkward "so, do you want kids?" on the third date. Everyone here has already answered.

Take the pledge

Confirm you're child-free by choice, sterilised, or a proud pet parent with no plans for kids. It's the one thing every member shares.

Meet them, and theirs

Profiles show the person and their animals. Wag at someone you like, or leave a note for their pet. Look for dating, friends, or both.

Join a pack

Walks, dinners, trips and game nights with grown-ups who get it. Bring your dog, or come solo. Start your own in a minute.

Questions

Good to know

Who is Unkittered for?

Adults (18+) who are child-free by choice, sterilised, or happily raising animals instead of children, and who want to meet others who feel the same, for dating, friendship or both.

I don't have a pet. Can I still join?

Yes. Plenty of members are animal lovers without an animal of their own right now. Your profile just won't have a pet section until you add one.

What if I'm not sure about children?

Then Unkittered probably isn't the right place yet. Everyone here has made a firm decision, and that's what makes it work. You're welcome back any time.

Is it free?

Creating a profile, matching, chatting and joining packs are free. Optional Plus and Gold memberships add extras. Paws are an in-app currency for sending gifts.

Where is it available?

We're starting in Kenya, the United Kingdom and the United States, with more countries to follow.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to You, then Settings, then Delete account. You can also request deletion here without the app.
