Your data

Delete your account

You can delete your Unkittered account and the data linked to it at any time, in the app or by email. Deleting can't be undone.

In the app

The quickest way. It takes effect straight away.

  1. Open Unkittered and tap You.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You'll be signed out and your profile disappears.

Without the app

Uninstalled it, or can't sign in? Email us from the address on your account with the subject Delete my account. We'll confirm it's you, then delete your account within 7 days and email you when it's done.

unkittered@gmail.com

What gets deleted

DeletedWhen
Your profile, photos, animals, pledge and badgesHidden straight away; deleted from our live systems immediately, and uploaded files removed within 30 days
Your matches, messages, stories, reels and the packs you hostImmediately
Your verification selfie and documentsWithin 30 days
Your Paws balance and membershipsImmediately. Unused Paws are lost.

What we may keep

  • Records of payments, where tax or financial law requires it (up to 7 years).
  • Limited information about accounts banned for serious harm, to stop them coming back.
  • Messages you sent stay with anyone you sent them to only if they saved a copy outside the app.

Paying for Plus or Gold? Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in your store's subscription settings first.

Just want a break? You can pause your profile in Settings instead. Nobody will see you, and everything's there when you come back. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
