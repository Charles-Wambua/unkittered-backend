Support

How can we help?

Every message is read by a real person. We aim to reply within 2 working days, and sooner for safety reports.

In immediate danger? Call 999 or 112 in Kenya, 999 in the UK, or 911 in the US before contacting us.

Report a member

Fastest in the app: open their profile or chat, tap the menu, then Report. Or email us their name and what happened. Screenshots help.

Report by email

Privacy request

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Send it from the email on your account so we can confirm it's you.

Make a request

Anything else

Sign-in trouble, payments and Paws, a bug, an appeal, or an idea. Tell us your phone model and what you were doing.

Quick answers

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we'll send a 6-digit code to your email. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that button instead. No code after 10 minutes? Check your spam folder, then email us from that address.

How do I cancel Plus or Gold?

Memberships are billed by Google Play or the App Store, so cancel there: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

My M-Pesa top-up hasn't arrived

Top-ups usually land within a minute of entering your PIN. If it's been longer, pull down to refresh your wallet. Still missing? Email us the M-Pesa confirmation code.

Why was my account suspended?

Usually because of reports that it broke our Community Guidelines. If you think we got it wrong, email us from your account address and a person will review it.

How do I get the Verified badge?

Go to You → Settings → Verification & badges and take a selfie. A person checks it against your photos, usually within a day.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You → Settings → Delete account. Or follow the steps on our delete account page.
