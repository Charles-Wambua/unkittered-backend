Community Guidelines

Unkittered works because people show up as themselves and treat each other well. These are the rules for profiles, chats, stories, reels and packs. Break them and you may lose your account.

Last updated 31 October 2026

01Be real

  • Use your own name and recent photos where your face is clearly visible.
  • Be honest about your age, your pledge and your animals. Everyone here took the same pledge in good faith.
  • One account per person. No impersonation, fake profiles or shared accounts.
  • You must be 18 or over. No photos of children, including your nieces, nephews or friends' kids.

02Be kind

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking or intimidation.
  • No hate speech or slurs about race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
  • "No" means no. Accept unmatches, declined plans and unanswered messages gracefully.
  • Don't share anyone's private information, photos or messages without their permission.

03Respect choices

Unkittered is a place where nobody has to defend being child-free. Don't shame people for their choices, try to change their minds about children, or mock parents either. Kindness goes both ways.

04Keep it clean

  • No nudity, sexually explicit content, or unsolicited sexual messages or photos.
  • No graphic violence, gore, self-harm content, or content promoting illegal drugs.
  • No content that sexualises or endangers minors, ever. We report it to the authorities.
  • No spam, chain messages or repeated unwanted messages.

05Animals

  • Never post content showing animal cruelty, neglect or fighting.
  • No buying, selling or breeding animals through Unkittered. Rehoming must go through a registered shelter or rescue.
  • If you bring an animal to a pack or date, keep it under control, vaccinated and welcome. Respect "humans only" packs.

06No scams or selling

  • Never ask other members for money, gift cards, crypto, or financial help, for any reason.
  • No advertising, promotion, recruiting, or soliciting, including for sex work or escort services.
  • Don't trade Paws or gifts for money or favours outside the app.

07Packs

  • Hosts: describe your pack honestly, choose a public meeting place, and cancel early if plans change.
  • Guests: only RSVP if you mean to come, and respect the pack's pet policy.
  • Keep phone numbers and social handles out of group chats. Share them one-to-one when you're ready.
  • Packs are not for selling, recruiting, or commercial events.

08Offline behaviour

These rules apply when you meet in person too. If a member hurts, threatens or harasses someone off the app, report it. We can act on behaviour that happens offline.

09How we enforce

Every report is read by a person. Depending on what happened, we may remove content, give a warning, limit features, suspend an account, or ban someone permanently. Serious harm, threats, or anything involving minors may be reported to the police.

Use Report on any profile or chat in the app, or contact us. The person you report isn't told who reported them.
