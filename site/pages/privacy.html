The short version

  • We collect what you put in your profile, your messages, and an approximate location, to help you meet people.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or trackers.
  • Other members never see your exact location, email or phone number.
  • You can download, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account removes it.

01Who we are

Unkittered ("we", "us") runs the Unkittered app and this website. We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the data controller under the UK GDPR and Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Questions or requests about your data go to {{email}}.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location. If you allow it, your phone shares its coarse location. We round it to about 100 metres before storing it, and we never ask for precise GPS.
  • Device and push token. A token that lets us send you notifications, and whether you're on Android or iPhone.
  • Activity status. When you were last active, if you choose to show it.
  • Server logs. Our web server records technical details like IP addresses and request times, for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising IDs, third-party analytics, or tracking pixels.

03How we use it

  • To create your account and show your profile to people you might like.
  • To suggest matches, filter by distance and preferences, and run packs, chats and stories.
  • To verify members and award trust badges.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, detecting abuse and spam, and enforcing our Community Guidelines.
  • To process memberships, Paws purchases, gifts and payouts.
  • To send notifications you've switched on, like new matches and messages.
  • To answer your questions and keep the service working.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We don't make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects using only automated means. Match suggestions are automated, but you choose who you talk to.

04What other members see

Your profile is meant to be seen, so other members can see your name, age, photos, bio, interests, animals, pledge path, trust badges and approximate distance.

  • They never see your email, phone number, exact location or payment details.
  • Distance is approximate, and the map shows everyone on a blurred grid of about 2 km. You can hide your distance completely.
  • Your sterilisation document is seen only by our review team, never by members. Members see only the badge.
  • Messages are visible to the people in that chat. Stories disappear from view after 24 hours.
  • You can pause your profile or go incognito at any time in Settings.

05Sensitive information

Some of what you share counts as sensitive data in law. Your pledge and sterilisation status relate to your health and family choices, and using a dating app may reveal information about your sex life.

We only process this information because you choose to give it to us, with your explicit consent, and only to run Unkittered. You can withdraw that consent by removing the information from your profile or deleting your account. If you're in Washington, Nevada or another US state with consumer health data laws, this section is our consumer health data notice: we collect it for the purposes above, share it only as described in section 07, and never sell it.

08Where it's stored

Our servers are in the European Union (Belgium). If you use Unkittered from Kenya, the US or elsewhere, your data is transferred there. The EU is recognised by the UK as providing adequate protection. For transfers from Kenya we rely on your consent and on the safeguards in this policy, as the Data Protection Act allows.

10Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access a copy of your data, in a portable format.
  • Correct anything that's wrong (most of it you can edit in the app).
  • Delete your data. See how to delete your account.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent.

Email {{email}} from the address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Kenya

You have the rights in the Data Protection Act, 2019. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

United Kingdom

You have the rights in the UK GDPR, including portability and the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

United States

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws have the rights to know, access, correct, delete and port their data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't sell data or do targeted advertising, so there's nothing to opt out of, but you can still make any request by email. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and contact your state Attorney General if you disagree with the outcome.

11Security

Connections to Unkittered are encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to our servers is limited to the people who run Unkittered. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password. If a breach puts you at risk, we'll tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

12Adults only

Unkittered is only for people aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn a member is under 18, we delete their account. If you think a minor is using Unkittered, please tell us.
